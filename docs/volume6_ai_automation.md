# Volume 6 – AI & Automation

## 1. Smart Content Copilot
*   **Biography Draft Generator**: Contributors can enter key bullet points (e.g. accomplishments, birth year, field) and receive a high-quality, engaging draft biography within the CMS panel.
*   **Automated Translation**: Dynamic translation pipeline (using translation APIs) to instantly swap content between English and Urdu language variants.

## 2. Advanced Search & Semantic Matching
*   **Vector Search**: Incorporating MongoDB Atlas Vector Search or a custom embedding strategy to deliver semantic query matching (e.g., matching "famous doctors who saved lives" directly to Dr. Ruth Pfau).
*   **Recommendations Engine**: Relates personalities and articles based on text similarity embeddings to keep readers engaged on detail pages.

## 3. Media Prompt Assistant
*   CMS automatically suggests optimized image prompts for DALL-E or Midjourney to create high-quality hero banners representing historical events or personalities.
